Toronto waterproofing contractors offer a wide range of services tailored to different water intrusion problems:

Exterior Waterproofing

Excavation and sealing of foundation walls to prevent water penetration from outside.

Interior Waterproofing

Interior sump pumps, drainage systems, and interior sealing for water that enters the basement.

Crack Repair & Injection

Concrete crack sealing and epoxy/polyurethane injection for active water leaks.

Sump Pump Installation

Primary and backup sump pump systems for water removal and basement moisture control.

Drainage & Weeping Tile

Foundation drainage systems and weeping tile installation to divert water away from the basement.

Underpinning & Lowering

Foundation strengthening (underpinning) or basement floor lowering for deeper headroom.

Different companies specialize in different service combinations. Some offer full-service scope, while others focus on specific areas. Review company profiles above to see which services each contractor specializes in.

Warranty terms vary significantly among Toronto waterproofing companies. This is one of the most important factors to evaluate when choosing a contractor.

Key Questions to Ask:
  • Is the warranty transferable to new owners?
  • Does the warranty cover labor and materials?
  • Are there any conditions or exclusions?
  • What is the exact duration? (Lifetime, 25 years, 10 years, etc.)

Common warranty types among our top-ranked companies:

  • Lifetime Transferable Warranty: RCC Waterproofing, DryShield, and others offer lifetime protection that transfers to new property owners
  • 25-Year Transferable Warranty: Direct Waterproofing offers a 25-year transferable warranty
  • Written/Transferable Warranty: Many companies offer written warranties with varying terms

Always get warranty details in writing before signing a contract. Long-term warranties from reputable companies are a sign of confidence in their work quality.

Choosing the right waterproofing company requires comparing multiple factors. Here's a systematic approach:

Step-by-Step Selection Process:
  1. Define Your Project Scope: Identify whether you need interior-only, exterior, or comprehensive waterproofing
  2. Check Operating History: Look for companies with 10+ years of experience serving Toronto
  3. Review Verified Ratings: Check Google reviews, HomeStars, and BBB ratings for real customer feedback
  4. Verify Licensing & Insurance: Ensure WSIB coverage, liability insurance, and proper licensing
  5. Compare Warranty Terms: Get warranty details in writing, noting duration and transferability
  6. Get Multiple Quotes: Request free estimates from 3-4 companies for accurate pricing
  7. Check References: Ask for customer references, especially from recent projects similar to yours

Evaluation Criteria to Compare:

✓ Service Quality Indicators
  • Free inspections offered
  • Detailed written estimates
  • Professional crew behavior
  • Project cleanup included
✓ Business Reliability
  • Local phone number
  • Established office location
  • Professional website
  • Emergency availability

Pro Tip: Don't automatically choose the cheapest quote. Waterproofing is a long-term investment in your home. Focus on companies that offer the best combination of experience, reputation, warranty coverage, and fair pricing.

Waterproofing costs in Toronto vary widely based on project scope, basement size, access, soil conditions, and the specific type of waterproofing needed.

Typical Cost Ranges:
  • Interior Sump Pump System: $2,000–$5,000
  • Exterior Waterproofing: $5,000–$15,000+ (depends on foundation size)
  • Foundation Crack Repair: $500–$2,000 per crack
  • Full Basement Waterproofing: $10,000–$30,000+ (comprehensive solution)

Factors that affect pricing:

  • Basement size and foundation depth
  • Severity of water damage or leaks
  • Accessibility for excavation
  • Local soil and water table conditions
  • Type of waterproofing system selected
  • Additional work (sump pumps, drainage, etc.)
Get Multiple Quotes

Always obtain free estimates from multiple companies. Compare pricing, warranty terms, and scope of work. Be wary of quotes that are significantly lower than others—this may indicate lower quality materials or workmanship.
