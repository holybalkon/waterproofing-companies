Waterproofing FAQ – Toronto

Common questions about basement waterproofing, foundation repair, and finding the right contractor

What is basement waterproofing and why do I need it?

Basement waterproofing is a specialized trade that protects your foundation, basement walls, and structure from water infiltration and damage. Toronto's high water table, clay soil composition, and freeze-thaw cycles make waterproofing essential for preventing basement flooding, mold, foundation cracks, and structural damage.

What are the main types of waterproofing?

Exterior waterproofing: Digging out around the foundation and applying sealant or membrane to the outside. Highly effective but more disruptive and expensive.

Interior waterproofing: Installing sump pumps, drainage systems, and sealants inside the basement. Less invasive but works in conjunction with other methods.

Crack repair: Injecting epoxy or polyurethane into foundation cracks to seal them.

Underpinning: Strengthening or deepening the foundation structure.

Backwater valves: Preventing sewage backup during heavy rainfall or flooding.

How much does waterproofing cost in Toronto?

Costs vary widely depending on the scope and contractor:

  • Crack repair: $500–$2,000
  • Interior waterproofing: $5,000–$15,000
  • Exterior waterproofing: $10,000–$25,000+
  • Underpinning: Can exceed $50,000

Get multiple quotes from licensed contractors and verify what is included in each estimate.

What warranty terms should I expect?

Many Toronto contractors offer lifetime transferable warranties that pass to new property owners when you sell. Some offer 10, 25, or other fixed-year terms. It's important to verify:

  • Coverage scope (does it cover materials, labor, or both?)
  • Conditions (e.g., annual maintenance requirements)
  • Transferability to future owners
  • What's excluded

Get warranty terms in writing before hiring.

How do I choose the right waterproofing contractor?

Compare contractors on:

  • Years operating in Toronto (longer history = more experience)
  • Verified platform reviews (Google, HomeStars, BBB)
  • Warranty terms and transferability
  • Service range (do they handle your specific needs?)
  • WSIB and liability insurance coverage
  • Free inspection and written estimate availability
  • Local references you can contact
  • Competitive pricing (get 3+ quotes)

Prioritize licensed, insured contractors with strong local reviews.

What is WSIB and why does it matter?

WSIB (Workplace Safety and Insurance Board) coverage ensures workers are insured for injuries that occur on the job. Hiring a contractor without WSIB coverage leaves you liable if a worker is injured. Always verify current WSIB coverage and liability insurance before work begins. Ask for proof.

How long does waterproofing work take?

Project duration varies depending on scope and conditions:

  • Crack repair: 1–2 days
  • Interior waterproofing: 3–7 days
  • Exterior waterproofing: 5–14 days (depending on foundation size and weather)
  • Underpinning: Several weeks

Ask your contractor for a realistic timeline and whether they need weather-dependent conditions.

Can I waterproof my basement myself?

Minor interior sealing can be DIY, but professional work should not be: professional exterior waterproofing, crack injection, underpinning, and sump pump installation require specialized equipment, expertise, and licensing. Improper work can cause costly damage. Hire licensed professionals for anything beyond minor repairs.

What are the signs I need waterproofing?

Watch for these warning signs:

  • Water stains or seepage on basement walls or floors
  • Mold or mildew odors
  • Efflorescence (white mineral deposits on concrete)
  • Foundation cracks
  • Bowing or leaning walls
  • Sump pump running frequently or continuously

Any of these warrant a professional inspection to assess the severity and recommend solutions.

Does Toronto offer any waterproofing assistance programs?

Yes. Toronto's Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program offers rebates for:

  • Sump pumps
  • Backwater valves
  • Foundation sealing
  • Other approved flood-prevention measures

Check Toronto.ca for current eligibility criteria and application details. Some contractors also offer financing options.
