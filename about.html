About Top Waterproofing Companies

Editorial standards, methodology, and transparency

Our Mission

Top Waterproofing Companies is an independent editorial resource designed to help property owners in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area find reliable, qualified waterproofing contractors. We provide unbiased research and transparent comparisons to support informed decision-making.

Editorial Standards

How We Rank Companies

We evaluate waterproofing contractors based on the following criteria:

  • Operating History: Years in business and track record in the Toronto market
  • Service Range: Breadth of services including exterior waterproofing, interior waterproofing, crack repair, sump pumps, drainage solutions, underpinning, and foundation work
  • Warranty Terms: Type, duration, transferability, and conditions of warranty coverage
  • Insurance and Licensing: WSIB compliance, liability insurance, and trade licensing
  • Verified Reviews: Platform ratings on Google, HomeStars, and BBB with review counts and recency
  • Reputation and Awards: Industry recognition and customer feedback

What Our Ratings Mean

The editorial ratings displayed on this site are our independent assessments, not platform ratings. We assign ratings on a scale of 1–5 based on our evaluation against the criteria above. These ratings are distinct from and should not be confused with Google reviews, HomeStars ratings, or BBB ratings.

Live Platform Data

Where we reference live platform data (Google reviews, HomeStars ratings, BBB accreditation), we label these clearly with tags like "LIVE PLATFORM" or "NOTE." We encourage visitors to verify this information directly on those platforms, as numbers and ratings may change.

Company Information

Detailed descriptions, facts, and focus areas for each company are based on publicly available information from their official websites, platform profiles, and published claims. We include caveats where information is company-stated (not independently verified) and where data may be outdated.

Transparency & Disclosures

We are committed to transparency. This site is maintained by a digital publishing team with expertise in home improvement and contractor research. We do not accept payment from companies for ranking placement. Links to contractor websites use standard affiliate or referral tracking where applicable and legally required disclosures are made.

Geographic Scope

Our primary focus is on contractors serving Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), including Mississauga, Brampton, Oakville, and surrounding municipalities. We are currently developing resources for other Ontario cities.

Updates & Changes

This site was last updated in October 2026. We periodically review and update company information, platform ratings, and our methodology. Material changes are reflected in the update date and, where significant, may be noted in our updates section.

Contact & Feedback

We value accuracy and fairness. If you notice factual errors, outdated information, or have questions about our methodology, please contact us. Contractor representatives may submit verified updates to their company information.

Legal Disclaimer

This editorial site provides information for educational and comparative purposes. Ratings and recommendations do not constitute professional advice or endorsements. Users are encouraged to conduct their own research, verify current information directly with companies, and consult qualified professionals before making hiring decisions. We are not responsible for contractors' work quality, performance, or disputes.
